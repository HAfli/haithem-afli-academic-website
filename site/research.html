Research

My research sits at the intersection of human-centred AI, multilingual language technology and reliable evaluation, with applications in health, biology and scientific discovery.

Dedicated research programmes

Cultural Adaptation in NLP

Research on culturally grounded language models, multilingual cultural reasoning and human-aligned evaluation.

Recent work: CRaFT (LREC 2026), Structural vs Cultural Reasoning (AACL 2026), and PalmX 2025.

Open the research site →

Beyond the Benchmark

Dynamic and distribution-based evaluation in NLP, from stochastic paraphrasing to multidimensional robustness and Measurement-as-Distribution.

Recent work: PTEB (EACL 2026), HTEB (EMNLP 2026), and Measurement-as-Distribution (EMNLP 2026 Findings).

Current research agenda

  1. Inclusive multilingual language models — reliable performance across high-resource and under-resourced languages.
  2. Cultural reasoning in generative AI — culturally specific knowledge, values and reasoning patterns.
  3. Reliable multilingual evaluation — limitations of automated judges, benchmarks and evaluation protocols.
  4. Inclusive translation technologies — preserving meaning, cultural context, sentiment and specialist terminology.
  5. Human-centred AI evaluation — combining expert, community and user evaluation with technical metrics.
  6. AI for health and scientific domains — language models, genomic foundation models and trustworthy machine learning.
