Teaching

Teaching that connects research in multilingual, trustworthy and human-centred AI with university education, professional training and public engagement.

Emphasises reproducibility, ethical awareness, interpretability, Universal Design for Learning (UDL), and critical engagement with AI in society.

University teaching · Dedicated teaching websites · Teaching innovation and outreach

University teaching

  • MSc in Computational Biology (MTU) — Co-led establishment; teaches Machine Learning for Biology (2021–present)
  • MSc in Data Analytics (MTU) — Applied Machine Learning (2019–present)

Dedicated teaching websites

Selected modules and training programmes have their own course websites with lecture materials and learning resources.

COMP9085

Machine Learning in Biology

MSc module · Munster Technological University

Dedicated course website for the MSc module COMP9085: a twelve-week journey from machine-learning fundamentals to foundation models for biological, health and nutrition data, with weekly lecture materials, practical labs, a dataset catalogue and interactive figures computed on real data in the browser.

Weekly lecture materials · Practical labs · Dataset catalogue · Interactive figures

Údarás na Gaeltachta

Building AI Confidence in the Gaeltacht Workplace

Professional training · delivered through Irish

Course website for an AI literacy and workplace training programme developed for Irish speakers and business owners in the Gaeltacht, delivered through the medium of Irish. It covers an introduction to AI, generative AI and large language models, practical applications in everyday work, AI safety and responsible use, AI literacy and the EU AI Act, and the role of AI in supporting the Irish language.

Session materials · Practical exercises · EU AI Act guide · Irish and English interface

Teaching innovation and outreach

  • AI education and professional training — Executive education in AI, Python and data analytics for European Central Bank staff (via Krisolis).
  • AI literacy and practical generative AI — Hands-on training in using generative AI and large language models safely and effectively at work.
  • Irish-language AI training — Irish-medium AI literacy training for Gaeltacht businesses, delivered by MTU for Údarás na Gaeltachta.
  • Teaching and learning innovation — Interactive, open course websites with accessible text alternatives for every figure, and clear labelling of real versus simulated data.

Connections with research

  • Machine Learning in Biology draws on research in AI for healthcare, biology and genomics.
  • The Údarás na Gaeltachta programme is an example of knowledge transfer, linking research on multilingual and culturally aware AI and responsible AI with AI literacy in Irish-speaking communities. See also Innovation & Industry.
